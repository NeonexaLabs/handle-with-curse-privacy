In short

  • The game is for everyone, children included. It never asks how old you are, and it treats every player as a child.
  • The game keeps your progress and settings on your phone. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no server of ours.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Because every player is treated as a child, the ads are not personalised, are suitable for all ages, and the game does not read your phone's advertising ID.
  • The game counts simple things, like days played and ads watched, to find out what to fix. They go to Google's Firebase Analytics, hold no name or contact detail, and you (or a parent) can switch them off in Settings.
  • "Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Usage statistics

While "Usage statistics" in Settings is ON (it is on until you switch it off), the game counts what happens in it and sends the counts to Google Firebase Analytics. We use them only to balance the game and fix problems, for example to see on which day players give up. The counts are:

  • the start and end of a shop day: the day number, whether it was a full-moon day, the booster used, the result (completed or not), the coins taken in and the number of explosions;
  • that an ad offer was shown, tapped and rewarded (the reward and its size), and that an ad failed to load or show, with a short error code;
  • that a between-days ad was shown;
  • coin purchases of antiques, upgrades and boosters (which one, its level and its price in coins) and which antique was unlocked;
  • that Remove ads was bought (not who bought it, nor how much was paid);
  • that the game was updated (the old and the new version number).

Firebase Analytics also records some things on its own, such as the first start of the game, the length of a session and the game's version, and collects technical information from your phone: the make and model, the Android version, the language, and the general area (country or region) worked out from the network address. Google says Analytics does not log or store individual IP addresses. All of it is tied to a random number that Firebase makes for this installation of the game. It is not your name, your Google account or your phone's advertising ID, and a new installation gets a new number.

What we do not send: your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, files, the advertising ID, or anything you type. We do not use these statistics for ads, we do not link them to other data, and the advertising features of Firebase and Google Analytics are switched off for this game. We keep them for no longer than 2 months.

Switch "Usage statistics" OFF (Settings > Privacy and credits) and the game stops counting and tells Firebase to stop collecting; nothing more is sent. A parent can do this for a child at any time. Because the counts carry no name and only a random installation number, we cannot tell whose they are, so we cannot look them up or delete one person's counts afterwards; switching them off and uninstalling the game ends the link, and old counts disappear on their own after the period above.

Google acts for us in this: it stores and processes the counts for us under its terms for Firebase and Google Analytics.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch in return for a reward, and full-screen ads between shop days. Buying Remove ads switches off the full-screen ones; the ads you choose to watch stay.

Every ad request is marked as directed to children and to users under the age of consent, ads are limited to the General Audiences rating, and personalised ads are turned off. That means no ads based on your interests or on what you did in other apps. The game does not ask Android for your advertising ID, and the game's package does not hold the permission to read it. There is no consent form for ads, because nothing needs your consent: personalised ads are never on.

To show, count and protect ads, Google's Mobile Ads software in the game still sends some technical information to Google, encrypted and automatically. The list follows Google's own published description of that software:

  • your IP address, which Google may use to estimate the general location of the phone;
  • how you use the app and the ads, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • device identifiers that are not the advertising ID, such as an app set ID.

We do not receive this information and we add nothing of our own to it. Google is responsible for what it does with it: see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices: you can manage the ads Google shows you in your Google account's ad settings, called My Ad Center (myadcenter.google.com), and in Android's settings (usually under Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads). Neither changes the ads in this game, which are never personalised.

Purchases

Remove ads is bought through Google Play. Google takes the payment and shows its own purchase screen; we never see your card or payment details. Google tells the game whether the purchase was paid, and the game keeps one yes-or-no value on your phone. We do not keep purchase tokens, order numbers or your Google account details. "Restore purchases" asks Google Play which purchases belong to the Google account on your phone. The coins in the game are only a game currency: they cannot be bought, and they are not money.

Children

The game is meant for everyone, children included, and every player is treated as a child. It never asks your age and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information, and children can play without any account. It has no chat, no user-generated content and no links to social networks. The ads are made for all ages and are not personalised. The only information that leaves the phone is the usage statistics described above (which a parent can switch off in Settings) and the technical information of Google's advertising software described above.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, or think a child has sent us personal information, write to us at the address above and we will answer and, where we can, act on it.

Where information goes and how it is protected

Information collected by Google's software (advertising and Firebase Analytics) goes to Google, which may process it in any country where it operates. It is sent encrypted (TLS). The game's own files stay in the game's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read on a normal, unmodified phone. No way of storing or sending information is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Keeping and deleting information

  • On your phone: until you use Reset save (progress only), clear the app's storage or uninstall the game.
  • Usage statistics: for no longer than 2 months (see above). Switch them off in Settings to stop new ones.
  • Advertising information: Google holds it under its own policies and retention periods. We keep no copy, so we cannot delete it for you. You can manage your Google account's ad data with the links above.
  • Emails to us: kept as long as needed to answer you and deal with the matter, then deleted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, California and other US states), you may have rights over personal information about you: to see it, correct it, delete it, limit or object to its use, take a copy, withdraw consent and complain to your data protection authority. The game gives us no name or contact detail, and the usage statistics carry only a random installation number, so there is almost nothing for us to look up or delete; you can stop new statistics by switching them off in Settings. For the information Google's advertising software collects, Google holds it and its tools (the links above) are how to use these rights. If you are not sure where to start, write to us and we will help you find the right place.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a feature. The effective date above changes when we do, and a change that collects more information is published before the update that needs it is released.
